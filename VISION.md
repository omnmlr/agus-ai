# Vision

AGUS AI starts with Solana Android release assurance. It connects a specific build and device environment to a wallet callback, finalized chain result and verifiable report. The first opportunity is a recurring release check for teams that need to reproduce wallet handoff or transaction inconsistencies across Android environments.

Dedicated providers can add device and wallet combinations a single release team cannot keep in its lab. A pilot should measure incremental defect detection from naturally occurring OS, memory, responsiveness and network differences at comparable cost. Providers earn from accepted workloads at disclosed terms. Repeat bookings, completed work, hardware availability and operations cost determine whether a marketplace can sustain itself.

AI evaluation teams may need controlled, realistic task environments and comparable evidence across independently supplied agents. Versioned tasks, comparable device profiles, deterministic assertions and matched test budgets make results interpretable. Separately authorized trajectories could support training. Customer evidence stays private by default.

An everyday-phone pilot depends on demonstrated isolation, clear provider controls, safe software distribution and privacy review across devices. Dedicated lab devices are the current supply model. The compatibility history, trusted provider operations and customer-authorized evaluation data could become durable as coverage and repeat usage grow.

Find initial customers among developers preparing Solana Mobile and dApp Store releases. Test demand through three teams exercising their own APK, two repeat CI runs and a paid pilot or purchase commitment. Demonstrate the original AGUS Crypto issues as the first case study.
