# Alternatives and competitive position

Comparison is of publicly described offerings, not a complete product audit. No claim that competitors lack an unlisted feature.

| Alternative | Established strengths | AGUS entry hypothesis | Where incumbent wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| AWS Device Farm [R1] | Metered physical Android/iOS testing and remote access | Solana-aware tests plus qualified human assignments | Fleet operations, scale, broad framework support |
| BrowserStack [R2] | Real-device cloud, subscription tooling, automation | Wallet-specific outcomes and combined contributor campaigns | Coverage, mature workflows and enterprise support |
| Applause/uTest [R3] | Managed real-world testing, global contributors, payments | Self-serve Solana-specific release workflows and chain evidence | Crowd quality, relationships, global operations |
| Testlio [R4–R5] | Managed QA, human and automated payments testing | Blockchain-native evidence and developer-facing agent evaluation | Managed delivery, payment expertise and reporting |
| Scale [R6] | Expert data collection, trajectories, evaluations and RL environments | Real mobile transaction tasks with independently checkable outcomes | Buyer relationships, dataset operations and quality systems |
| AndroidWorld [R20] | Open benchmark with repeatable Android tasks | Customer-owned apps, physical configurations, human corrections, chain-grounded rewards | Reproducible research benchmark; free substitute for some evaluation |
| Solana Mobile SDK/Fake Wallet [R14–R16] | Free official development tools | Complement with real hardware and human studies | Authoritative SDK, low-cost development |
| Internal QA / community beta | Familiar app, no new vendor, full control | Reduce recruitment and repeat manual work | Sensitive builds, trust, low marginal cost for small tests |

## Differentiation to prove

A campaign should tie together exact build hash, explicit device/profile provenance, versioned instructions, authorized human or agent actions, deterministic evaluation, relevant chain verification and evidence integrity. For a failure, return reproduction conditions and recovery behavior. For several contributors, return aggregate findings with denominators and deduplication. The differentiated product is **the verified result**, not a logo, payment rail or raw hardware rental.

## Defensibility and threats

Possible compounding assets: opt-in compatibility history, verified device coverage, reliable contributor specialization, versioned test libraries and integrations. None is a proven moat yet. Incumbents can add crypto-specific features. AGUS must earn trust through repeatable results, privacy boundaries, cost and speed. A decentralized supply model can increase variety but also increases fraud, availability and isolation costs.

## Scope discipline

Start with a Solana wallet assurance and human usability package. Avoid competing on total device count, generalized penetration testing, or enterprise data licensing before quality and rights processes exist. Never imply a blockchain verification substitutes for security review.
