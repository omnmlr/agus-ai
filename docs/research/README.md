# AGUS research dossier

Research snapshot: 27 November 2026. Engineering baseline: [7a2ece4](https://github.com/omnmlr/agus-ai/commit/7a2ece43d60732c4d2b2a054d0997515f90e302d).

**Thesis:** AGUS connects application teams and AI developers to qualified Android devices and human contributors, starting with Solana Mobile. The deliverable is evidence that an application or agent actually behaves correctly—not merely access to a phone.

A developer without a Seeker can obtain authorized hardware testing; a developer who owns one can still benefit from diverse configurations, repeatable regression testing, independent chain verification, and structured human studies.

## What is real today?

The baseline [implementation ledger](../../PROGRESS.md) documents real Android emulator runs, Localnet wallet transfers, a false-success negative control, signed execution reports and a reconciled Localnet payout. It also documents synthetic-adapter Gemini actions and pending hosted activation, public Devnet payout, live identity delivery, physical Android and Seeker verification. These are repository-reported results, not tests rerun by this research author.

The expanded phone-native and human-campaign architecture is approved product direction, **not a claim of implementation**. The engineering team is investigating it independently. This dossier does not override engineering security decisions or promise unsupported device capabilities.

## Evidence labels

**External evidence:** primary-source descriptions, published research, public issue reports. **Hypothesis:** proposed AGUS pricing, market entry, partner interest or functionality. **Repository evidence:** a linked implementation or run with its actual scope. No customer interviews, contracts, revenue, partnerships, or actual willingness to pay for AGUS are asserted here.

For a judge with three minutes: read the Solana opportunity, customer use cases and economics; then inspect the demo and implementation ledger.
