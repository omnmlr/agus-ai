# Qualified contributor economy

The owner contributes either scheduled foreground device access or skilled human work. Background virtualization and simultaneous personal use are deferred. Ordinary supported participation should be phone-native, with explicit setup and safety qualification; dedicated hardware remains an alternative.

## Qualification as a product benefit

| Gate | Measurable evidence | Buyer value |
|---|---|---|
| Device eligibility | OS, model, hardware capabilities, runtime compatibility | Appropriate test matrix |
| Isolation eligibility | Versioned safety checks and verified execution class | Reduced personal data exposure |
| Network and resources | Measured connectivity, storage, battery/thermal state | Fewer interrupted jobs |
| Human fundamentals | Sample assignment with rubric and feedback | Consistent evidence and instructions |
| Specialization | Wallet concepts, bug reporting, language or accessibility skills | Better matching |
| Reliability | Completion, punctuality and quality history, sample size | Predictable delivery |

Do not create arbitrary degree requirements. Use relevant skills and allow retests. Do not equate expensive devices with quality: low-end models may be exactly what a buyer needs. Separate device capability from human reliability; avoid invented composite scores and small-sample overconfidence.

## Work types

**Automated:** explicit session consent, scope, duration, foreground screen, owner stop; never require user to reveal PIN or disable protections. **Interactive:** accept task, review instructions and compensation, launch approved app, submit task outcomes/evidence, review or appeal decision. **Mixed:** consented human correction or confirmation during a bounded automated test; wallet and biometric approvals stay with the owner. Do not guarantee overnight unattended work that requires repeated OS consent.

## Quality control

Version rubrics, include known-answer qualification tasks, check duplicate submissions and inconsistent timestamps, sample human review, compare independently checked outcomes, and record evidence provenance. Avoid blanket surveillance or collection of unrelated personal activity. Do not infer fraud solely from unusual hardware or a network interruption. Provide reason codes and appeal. Fraud-resistant identity and device integrity signals require privacy review; never claim attestation proves human skill.

## Compensation and safety

Show expected effort, fixed terms, acceptance rubric, review window, revisions and payout status before acceptance. A well-executed test that discovers a bug is valuable. Safety termination must always be available; don't discourage it with punitive ratings. Qualification should be proportionate, and requested rework should have fair compensation rules. No earnings simply for enrollment. No purchase of test funds, no personal wallet seed exposure, no custody of participant balances.

Device constraints: user-authorized time window, resource and thermal limits, charging/network policy, local fail-closed lease and emergency stop. Measure provider net experience and effective hourly earnings inclusive of waiting/rework; no income guarantees.

## Supply strategy

Recruit a small, diverse pool after proving the isolation model; gate listings by supported workloads. Do not acquire a large idle crowd before buyer demand. Compare device coverage, task fill rate, evidence acceptance, support cost and retention. The minimum credible marketplace is a small supply pool completing valuable, authorized work.
