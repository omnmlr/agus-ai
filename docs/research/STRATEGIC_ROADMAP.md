# Solana-first roadmap and judging narrative

## Product sequence

1. **Proof:** a complete wallet assurance journey on authorized device/emulator, honest chain evidence and a structured human assignment. Qualify phone-native participation only where verified. Demonstrate exact mode and limits.
2. **Pilot:** narrow Solana release package and usability campaigns, controlled contributor cohort and repeatable reports. Establish acceptance rubric, support and sustainable payment operations.
3. **Repeat:** CI/MCP recurring release checks, wider wallet/device matrix, reproducible bug campaigns, opt-in compatibility aggregation. Measure retention and margin.
4. **Expand:** mobile agent evaluation, human correction and commissioned rights-cleared data; only after quality/rights processes and real buyer requirements. Do not assume background virtualization is needed.

## Three-minute pitch outline

- 0:00–0:35: founder experience: building for Solana Mobile without comprehensive hardware/wallet access. Even owning one Seeker is not representative.
- 0:35–1:15: developer commissions a test, chooses exact build, device requirements and qualified human or automated assignment.
- 1:15–2:10: show actual available execution evidence, wallet callback vs independently verified chain effect; show a real human submission if implemented and verified.
- 2:10–2:45: evidence/report, contributor compensation state and limitations. Label Localnet/Devnet and emulator/physical explicitly.
- 2:45–3:20: repeat release assurance, ecosystem benefit and AI evaluation opportunity. Link research dossier and engineering acceptance report for depth.

Only show live functionality if verified. If phone-native or human campaigns are incomplete, identify them as proposed; never splice synthetic footage as live execution. Current repository evidence is in [PROGRESS.md](../../PROGRESS.md) and [DEMO.md](../../DEMO.md).

## Metrics for the next decision

Buyer: repeat purchase, defect yield, time to reproduction, report acceptance. Provider: eligible coverage, completion rate, paid utilization, effective earnings, safety stops and appeals. Platform: per-workload contribution margin, queue time, duplicate payment incidents, privacy/security incidents and successful cleanup. Agent: objective success and false-success rates on pinned tasks.

## Strategic risks

- **Isolation/distribution:** gate capability and avoid overpromising; do not rely on broad privileges.
- **Demand:** external market evidence is not AGUS traction; no outreach before submission required.
- **Liquidity:** recruit only enough qualified supply for real campaigns.
- **Trust:** do not claim signed evidence proves hardware or that an on-chain transaction proves all app behavior.
- **Payments:** Devnet only until reviewed real-money controls; no general-purpose custodial balances.
- **Competition:** sell specialized results before trying to be a universal device farm.
- **Economics:** review and rework can erase margin; measure and reprice.

No token launch, artificial passive-income rewards, or new infrastructure commitments are necessary to test this thesis.
